# Ghana Financial Inclusion Analysis

## Overview

This project examines patterns and determinants of financial inclusion in Ghana, with particular attention to access to and use of formal and digital financial services.

The project applies quantitative data analysis and visualisation techniques to explore differences across demographic and socioeconomic groups.

It is designed as an independent research and data-analysis project using publicly available data.

---

## Research Questions

The analysis will explore questions such as:

1. What are the major patterns of financial inclusion in Ghana?

2. How does financial inclusion vary across demographic groups?

3. Are there differences between rural and urban populations?

4. How does financial inclusion vary by gender?

5. What socioeconomic characteristics are associated with access to financial services?

6. What implications do the findings have for financial inclusion policy in Ghana?
